How it works

From thesis to ranked shortlist
in a single afternoon.

  1. 1

    Write the Query

    One paragraph in plain language. No Boolean queries, no controlled vocabularies - just describe what you'd brief an analyst with.

  2. 2

    Tune the scorecard

    Pick the dimensions that matter for this thesis - and weight them or let us decide from comparable past landscapes.

  3. 3

    Agents fan out

    Specialised agents query trial registries, IP offices, literature, and filings in parallel. Results are de-duplicated, normalised, and scored.

  4. 4

    Review with the evidence

    Every score links to its source. Drill from a number on a row to the exact clinical trial arm or patent claim that produced it.

  5. 5

    Stay in the loop

    Requery runs forever. You'll know within minutes when a new asset appears or a ranked one changes, with a one-line explanation of why.
